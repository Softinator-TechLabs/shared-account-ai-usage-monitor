# Privacy and policy

This system may collect highly sensitive work-session content. A workspace owner configures the policy; each device user sees and acknowledges that policy during connection. The server enforces the same policy for reads, search, exports, comments and MCP access.

- **Collection:** metadata-only or full session content, as explicitly configured.
- **Redaction:** an explicit setting; “none” means no hidden masking. Known-secret-pattern filtering is never a complete guarantee.
- **Visibility:** the configured audience determines who can read a session. Upload tokens do not grant read access.
- **Storage:** real transcripts, credentials, connection files and staff mappings belong in the private workspace, never the public source repository.

Before enabling full content for a team, agree on the workplace policy, retention and who can access it. Review the actual workspace policy text; this public page does not override it. Project leaders should discuss coaching with employees and keep human review in any employment or pay decision.
